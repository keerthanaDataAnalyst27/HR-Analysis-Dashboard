# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee data and provide valuable insights into workforce trends, employee attrition, job satisfaction, and organizational performance. The dashboard enables HR teams and management to make informed decisions using data-driven insights.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition can significantly impact organizational productivity and increase recruitment costs. This dashboard helps identify key factors contributing to attrition and supports strategic workforce planning.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Monitor workforce demographics
- Measure employee satisfaction levels
- Identify high-risk attrition groups
- Support HR decision-making through data visualization

---

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Employees | Total workforce count |
| Attrition Count | Number of employees who left |
| Attrition Rate | Percentage of employee turnover |
| Average Age | Mean employee age |
| Average Income | Mean monthly salary |
| Average Years at Company | Employee tenure analysis |

---

## Key Insights

- Employees in specific departments show higher attrition rates.
- Younger employees contribute significantly to employee turnover.
- Job satisfaction has a strong relationship with employee retention.
- Certain job roles experience more attrition compared to others.
- Employees with lower tenure tend to leave more frequently.
- Income and satisfaction levels influence retention trends.
